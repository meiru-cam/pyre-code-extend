This is the first round of the process: a 30-minute phone call with a recruiter, not an engineer. It combines a standard set of behavioral questions with an introduction to the logistics of the rest of the process — timeline, compensation expectations, and work location. The questions fall into four groups.

### Background

- Walk through your background, your resume, and your most recent project.
- Of everything you've worked on, which one are you proudest of, and why?
- Describe a project that was technically difficult, and what made it hard.

### Motivation and direction

- Why do you want to join OpenAI specifically, rather than another company?
- Which kind of work do you prefer: product-facing, infrastructure, or research?
- Why are you leaving your current position?
- What's your own view on AGI, and how do you think about AI safety?

### Leadership and conflict

- Have you led other people before? Describe how.
- Tell me about a conflict that came up on a project and how you handled it.

### Process and logistics

- What are your salary expectations?
- When could you start if you were offered the role?
- What other interview processes are you in, and what stage are they at?
- What is your preferred work location, and do you need visa sponsorship?
- Is there anyone who could speak to your work — a reference, or someone you know at the company?
