This is the first round of the process: a 30-minute call with a recruiter, not an engineer. A resume walkthrough is brief or skipped outright — the call opens directly on why you want to work at Anthropic, and a substantial share of the remaining time goes to AI safety and Anthropic's mission, at the depth of a first conversation rather than a full technical or values round. The recruiter also lays out the shape of the rest of the loop and what a reference check will require. Passing this call moves you forward, but it does not always schedule a technical round directly: a separate review of your background by the hiring manager can sit between this call and the first technical interview. The questions fall into four groups.

### Motivation

- Why Anthropic, specifically — not just an AI company, but this one, rather than another lab working on similar problems?
- Why do you want to work at an AI company at all, given where your career has been so far?
- Why are you looking for a new role right now, and why leave your current one?
- If you end up with more than one offer, what is your decision framework for choosing between them?
- What long-term career direction are you aiming for?

### Safety and mission

- What does AI safety mean to you, in your own words?
- How would you describe what sets Anthropic apart from other AI labs — in mission, research focus, product choices, or approach to safety?
- What do you see as the biggest risks and the biggest benefits of more capable AI, and what could go wrong if a system like that is misused?
- Name one AI-related problem you think is currently underrated.
- Point to one specific, recent piece of Anthropic's public work you have actually read — a paper, a blog post, a policy document such as the Responsible Scaling Policy — and say what you took from it.

### Background

- Walk through your background and your most recent or most relevant project.
- Which of your past projects — backend, infrastructure, distributed systems — line up best with what this role does, and how much hands-on experience do you have building that kind of system?
- For the project you consider most relevant: what was your part in it end to end, and how long did it take?
- If your title implies leading people (tech lead, manager), what does the role actually involve day to day, and — if the role you're applying for is an individual-contributor one — are you comfortable with that scope?

### Logistics

- What is your work-authorization status, and what location or locations can you work from?
- Which programming language would you use in the technical interviews?
- When could you start interviewing, and, separately, start the job if offered? What other interview processes are you in, at what stage, and do any of them have a decision deadline?
- What is your current level, what level are you targeting here, and what are your compensation expectations?
- Is there a specific kind of team or work you're hoping to be matched with, if the team isn't decided by this call?
